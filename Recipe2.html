Bicol Express ni Nunoy

Ingredients:

  • Coconut milk
  • 500g Pork belly
  • 8-10 pcs Siling Pangsigang or Finger Chillies
  • 1 Chopped Onion
  • 4 Cloves Garlic
  • 1 Thumb Ginger, Julienned
  • 3 tbsp Bagoong Alamang
  • Shrimp Paste
  • 1 tbsp Cooking oil
  • Salt & Pepper

Step by Step:

  1. Cook the pork: Heat oil in the pan. Add pork belly and cook until lightly browned and fat is rendered. Remove excess fat.
  2. Sauce aromatics: Add garlic, onion, and ginger. Sauté until fragrant. Add bagoong alamang and cook for 30 seconds.
  3. Simmer: Pour in thin coconut milk or water. Bring to a boil, then simmer for 10-12 minutes until pork is tender.
  4. Add gata and chilli: Pour in thick coconut milk. Add all the chillies, stir gently and simmer on low heat for 5-7 minutes. Avoid over stirring so the coconut milk won't curdle.
  5. Finish: Season with salt and pepper. Cook until sauce thickens and oil starts to float on top.
